# Research document format

Written the moment a source taught something (EVID-10), in the topic's folder, dated, never
overwriting an earlier one (DECIDE-5).

```markdown
# <Question answered>

Date: <date> · Topic: <folder> · Calibration: see each finding

## Question
<One sentence.>

## Sources
| Source | Read | Where |
|---|---|---|
| <document, transcript, page> | in full / partial (<which part>) | <link or path> |

## Method
<The exact commands or queries, so the work is never redone.>

## Findings
| # | Finding | Evidence | Calibration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | <one sentence> | <quote, number with query, file:line at sha> | Verified / Measured / Inferred |

## Open questions
| # | Question | Who can answer | By |
|---|---|---|---|
```

## Rules

- Say which sources were read in full and which were not (EVID-4).
- Quotes are verbatim only after they were compared with the source (EVID-3). Check speaker
  attribution before quoting a transcript.
- Numbers carry their query (EVID-5).
- No individuals named; roles and systems (DECIDE-4).
